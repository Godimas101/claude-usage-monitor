NOSTROMO ICONIC

Default theme. Amber P3 phosphor, chunky pip bars, chamfered hardware panels and a dashboard that boots like a ship's computer. Widgets stay flat so Pillow can draw them; only the dashboard uses bloom, scanlines and tube curvature.

01

FLOATING WIDGET

280 px wide · 94 px (2-row) and 126 px (3-row) at 100% · chamfer 10 px · desktop wallpaper behind

FRAME
280 x 126, origin top-left. Every Y below is frame-relative. Chamfer 10 px on top-left and bottom-right. 1 px edge outline, 1 px top highlight.
HEADER (Y 6-18)
Share Tech Mono 10 px, tracking 0.14 em. Status word left, state word right.
ROW (Y 30 / 62 / 94)
Label 10 px at +0. Value VT323 22 px right-aligned. Rail at +14. Pip bar at +17, 8 px high.
PIP BAR
252 px span. 14 segments of 14 px with 4 px gaps. Unlit segments use --ghost. Partial segment is clipped at the fill edge.
TIME RAIL
1 px dotted full window. Solid --dim to the elapsed fraction. 2 x 5 px --hot tick marks "now".
PACE
Fill past the tick = ahead of time. Fill short of the tick = under pace.
GLOW
Gaussian blur r 3 px on lit pips and values, added under the crisp layer. Scanlines: 1 px every 3 px at 30% black.
STATES
WARN: pips to --bright, value to --hot. CRIT: --hot pips, inverted state chip, 4 px hazard band, value blinks. STALE: tokens dimmed, rows 1-2 kept, message box in row 3.

STRIP
Height 34 px inside the 48 px taskbar. Chamfer 8 px. Width shrink-wraps the content, about 280 px.
BARS
5H and 7D, 60 px wide, 10 pips of 4 px with 2 px gaps. Fill = usage. No time rail at this size.
DOT
6 px, --bright, blinks at 2.4 s while LIVE. STALE = hollow ring. Keeps the alive indicator on the strip.
FIGURES
VT323 18 px for percentages, Share Tech Mono 10 px for 5H and 7D. Right-aligned, tabular.
TASKBAR
Win11 dark, 48 px. Tray icon sits left of the strip. Clock is two lines, Segoe UI Variable Text 12 px.

TRAY ICON

16 / 24 / 32 px · ring = session % clockwise from 12 o'clock · centre dot = status

GEOMETRY
Outer radius 7.6 / 11.6 / 15.6 px. Ring thickness 3.2 / 4.2 / 5.2 px. Centre dot radius 1.7 / 2.6 / 3.6 px. Pixel centres decide ink, no anti-aliasing.
ARC
Starts at 12 o'clock and runs clockwise to pct x 360 degrees. Track is --dim. Warn uses --bright for the arc, crit uses --hot, stale uses --dim with no centre dot.
READABILITY
At 16 px the arc keeps 3 px of thickness, so the 13% wedge stays visible. Do not add numerals at 16 px; they do not survive the size.
