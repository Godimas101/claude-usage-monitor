PHOSPHOR CONSOLE

Default theme. Amber P3 phosphor on CRT black, segmented pip bars, chamfered hardware panels and a dashboard that types its boot log once per session. Widgets are flat and Pillow-safe. Only the dashboard uses bloom, scanlines and tube curvature. Every colour is a token; variants swap them with one data-variant attribute.

01

FLOATING WIDGET

280 px wide · 126 px with the FABLE row, 94 px without · 100 percent scale · desktop wallpaper behind

FRAME
280 x 126 (3 rows) or 280 x 94 (2 rows). Origin top-left, every Y below is frame-relative. Chamfer 10 px outer, 9.4 px inner, top-left and bottom-right. 1 px --edge border.
HEADER (y 6 to 18)
Alive dot 6 px at x 14. Status pill 12 px high at x 26, 1 px --rule-strong outline, 10 px SemiBold. Age after the pill, 10 px. Severity word right-aligned at x 266.
ROWS (y 28 / 60 / 92)
Name at x 14, 10 px SemiBold, --main. Qualifier 6 px after it, 10 px Regular, --label. Value in VT323 22 px, right edge x 266, cap top y row-2, always a full-size percent sign.
TIME RAIL (row + 17)
1 px. Dotted (2 on, 2 off) --ghost across x 14 to 266 is the whole window. Solid --label to the elapsed fraction. 2 x 5 px --hot tick at row + 15 to row + 20, centred on the elapsed x.
PIP BAR (row + 21 to row + 29)
16 cells, 12 px wide, 4 px gaps, x 14 to 266, 8 px high. Lit --main, unlit --ghost. The boundary cell fills from the left by its fraction.
PACE
Lit cells past the tick are ahead of pace. Lit cells short of the tick are under pace. The dashboard prints the numbers.
GLOW
Lit cells get a 3 px blur at 22 percent. Values get a 2 px blur at 60 percent and a 7 px blur at 22 percent. Crisp layer on top. Scanlines: 1 px every 3 px at 30 percent black.
STATES
WARN: lit cells and state word to --bright, value to --hot. CRIT: session value is a static inverted chip (--bright plate, --ink text), session cells --hot, 4 px hazard band, no blink. STALE: all tones to --stale, dashed auth box in row 3, dot becomes a hollow ring. OFFLINE and PAUSED: two-row frame, PAUSED shows a pause glyph instead of a dot.

TASKBAR STRIP

264 x 34 px inside a 48 px Windows 11 taskbar · left of the tray icon and clock · labels 11 px

4:12 PM2026-10-21
STRIP
264 x 34 px. Chamfer 8 px outer and 7.4 px inner, top-left and bottom-right. Background plate to CRT gradient, 30 percent scanlines. Shown on the taskbar at the same height.
ROW ORDER
Dot x 10, y 14. 5H label x 22. 5H bar x 44 to 102. 5H value right edge x 126. Divider x 134. 7D label x 144. 7D bar x 166 to 224. 7D value right edge x 256. All on one line, y 7 to 27.
BARS
10 cells, 4 px wide, 2 px gaps, 8 px high, at y 13. Same fill rule as the widget: lit cells --main, unlit --ghost, boundary cell filled by fraction.
TYPE
Labels: IBM Plex Mono SemiBold 11 px, --label. Percentages: VT323 18 px, --hot with a 2 px and 5 px glow.
DOT
6 px circle at x 10, y 14, --bright, blinks at 2.4 s while LIVE. STALE: hollow ring in --stale. Reduced motion: dot stays solid.
03

TRAY ICON

16 / 24 / 32 px · ring = session percent, clockwise from 12 o'clock · each icon carries its own dark plate

16 PX AT 8x
LIGHT TASKBAR, 16 PX
PLATE
Square of 16, 24 or 32 px, fill --crt, 1 px --edge border, chamfer 3 / 4 / 5 px top-left and bottom-right (inner 2.4 / 3.4 / 4.4 px). The plate makes the icon legible on a light taskbar too.
GEOMETRY
Centre at (S/2, S/2). Pixel centres decide ink, so no anti-aliasing at 16 or 24 px. Ring outer radius 6.0 / 9.5 / 14.0 px. Ring thickness 3.0 / 3.5 / 4.0 px. Track is --tray-track.
ARC
From 12 o'clock, clockwise, to percent x 360 degrees. Colour: --main normal, --bright warning, --hot critical.
CENTRE
16 px: 2 x 2 square at pixels 7 to 8. 24 px: 4 x 4 square at pixels 10 to 13. Both in the arc colour. 32 px: the percent in VT323 18 px, centred, in --hot.
STALE
No arc. The track is dashed (2 px on, 2 px off, measured along the mid radius) in --stale. No centre mark. At 32 px the centre reads "--". This gives stale its own silhouette without relying on tone.
CHECK
At 16 px the 13 percent arc is about 47 degrees, roughly 4 px of arc at mid radius. It is visible on both the dark and light test rows. Confirm once on a real 100 percent taskbar before the Pillow reference is frozen.

VARIANTS

eight variants cover the eight app accents · one data-variant attribute swaps every token · no component has a per-variant rule

AMBER P3Default. The current app colour, refined.
TERMINAL GREEN P1Green phosphor, terminal grade.
WHITE P4Neutral white, low glare.
RED ALERTAlert palette for hot runs.
BLUE BRIDGECool blue for bridge-style displays.
CYANCyan phosphor, cool and clinical.
ORANGEWarm orange, close to amber.
PURPLEViolet phosphor, low glare.

Token set per variant: --plate --crt-2 --hot --bright --main --label --dim --stale --ghost --ink --edge --edge-hi --rule --rule-strong --glow --glow-soft --tray-track. Chip order: hot, bright, main, label, dim, ghost, stale, crt. The app accent names map as Default to amber, Green to green, White to white, Red to red, Blue to blue, Cyan to cyan, Orange to orange, Purple to purple.

FONTSMichroma, IBM Plex Mono (SemiBold 600 and Regular 400) and VT323 are SIL Open Font License 1.1, free to bundle as static TTF. The taskbar clock uses Segoe UI Variable Text, a Windows system font that is not bundled.

CLOCKOne sample clock for the whole sheet: Wednesday 2026-10-21, 4:12 PM. Session window 2:39 PM to 7:39 PM, 31 percent elapsed. Weekly and FABLE window ends Saturday 4:00 PM, 57 percent elapsed.

SCOPEMock only. No logos, marks, franchise names or film assets. All copy is original. The theme codename is internal and not shown in product copy.

CAPTUREWhen the page is driven by automation it sets data-capture on the html element. Motion is then frozen to the ON frame, so static art shows the lit state. Product CSS does not depend on it.
