Night City v2 · review

Owner feedback applied: Edgerun now leans into yellow, Blackwall is renamed Blackwall and restyled after the quickhack UI (still the default), and the tray octagon is replaced by four options with a recommendation. BEFORE is the approved v1 render; AFTER is final.html v2. Images are screenshots of the mockup at 1x unless marked 2x.

01Edgerun

v1 was cream on dark with yellow as an accent. v2 makes yellow the dominant colour. The floating widget and the taskbar strip are yellow plates with ink text, ink RAM cells and a black LIVE chip with a cyan dot. The dashboard gets yellow title and card plates, a filled TIME REMAINING bar for each reset, and a Breach Protocol code matrix and buffer in lime (sampled from the reference at about #D6F55A). v1 had no full Edgerun dashboard, so its BEFORE thumbnail is the v1 overview recoloured for comparison.

03Tray options

The octagon is retired. Four alternatives, each drawn as shapes (Pillow-safe, with TTF digits on option D only), each on its own dark plate so it reads on a dark or light taskbar. Every option is shown at 16, 24 and 32 px, at 0, 13, 72 and 93 percent and STALE, for all four variants. Dark and light taskbar rows sit under each variant label.

04Changes by variant

  • Blackwall (default): id blackwall, name Blackwall. Red line art on red-black, cyan RAM cells and selection box, boxed RST and countdown, red REC-style dot, key-hint footer (R REFRESH, D DASHBOARD, ESC HIDE), small wireframe reticle on the nav.
  • Edgerun: yellow plates for the widget and strip (ink text, ink cells, black LIVE chip with cyan), yellow title bar and card title plates, TIME REMAINING bars, Breach code matrix and buffer in lime, yellow RAM cells in the tray.
  • Relic and Mox: colours and slanted bars unchanged. Tokens --nc-bar (usage fill) and --nc-sel (selection) are now set explicitly on both so no value leaks between variants.
  • Pillow rule: plates, cells, hexagon, octagon and slant geometry are polygons and rectangles; text is TTF; glow is the blur layer; no CSS-only tricks in the widget or tray. Fonts unchanged (SIL OFL 1.1); no logos or game art.
