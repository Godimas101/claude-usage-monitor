GATES · FINAL · MOCKUP v2 · widgets are Pillow-ready, dashboard is webui-ready

Readouts on native Fluent surfaces

A Quick Settings card that reads time against usage: a thin rail is the share of the window already gone, a bar is what you have spent, and the bar sits under the rail so pace is visible at a glance. Segoe UI Variable for text, Segoe Fluent Icons for glyphs, Cascadia Mono for numbers. Every colour is a token. A variant is one data-variant and one data-mode on the same element. All figures are fixtures derived from one clock: Wed 2026-10-21, 4:12 PM.

  • Time rail 2 px at y + 19, x 15 to 273: track time-track, fill time to the elapsed %, index tick 1 × 6 px at the fill end, from y + 17.
  • Usage bar 4 px at y + 23, radius 2: track, fill accent, usage-warn at 60 %, usage-alert at 85 %.
  • Fable share tick 1 × 10 px at x 144 from the bar top minus 3. Footer 11 px Regular at y 98 (line 16).

Rules

  • Severity from usage: warn at 60 %, alert at 85 % (app defaults, API severity wins when sent). Rails come from the reset timestamp: elapsed = (now − (reset − window)) / window.
  • Pace: ahead of pace when usage % is above elapsed %. Under pace otherwise.
  • Fable row shows only when the LIVE feed reports a Fable window with share above 0 % and session and weekly are both normal. Otherwise the footer takes the slot and the Fable row is dropped with it.
