Precision readouts on native Fluent surfaces

Time is a hairline, usage is the accent, status is a lamp, figures are tabular. Type is Segoe UI Variable (Small, Text, Display), glyphs are Segoe Fluent Icons, numbers in readouts are Cascadia Mono. Every colour is a token; a variant is one data-variant swap. All figures are sample values.

Pillow map at 100 %

  • Card: rounded_rectangle r 8, fill widget-bg, 1 px widget-border. Draw at 2x, LANCZOS down.
  • Shadow: rounded-rect mask offset 8 px down, GaussianBlur r 6, alpha .16 (light) / .46 (dark).
  • Header: 14 px line. Lamp = ellipse 6 px, halo ring 2 px at 24 % alpha. Status = 11 px SemiBold caps, +0.09 em tracking.
  • Label = 11 px SemiBold caps, tracking .07 em, text-dim. Tag = 10 px mono. Value = 15 px Cascadia Mono Bold, right aligned.
  • Time rail: 2 px line 3 px under the value line, time-track full width, time filled to the elapsed share. Index tick 1 x 6 px at its end.
  • Usage bar: 4 px pill (r 2) starting 2 px under the rail, track 12 % alpha, fill = accent, amber or red.
  • Row gap 6 px. Stale: rows at 42 % alpha, message row added (14 px + 6 px gap).

Reading the pace

  • Rail longer than the bar = behind pace (calm). Bar longer than the rail = ahead of pace.
  • Severity follows the usage bar colour and the value colour. The lamp only reports the link.
